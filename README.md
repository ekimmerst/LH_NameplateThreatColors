<h1 style="color: #d9a94b;">LH NameplateThreatColors</h1>

<p>Color Blizzard nameplate health bars by threat, with tank, damage, and healer modes for a quick view of enemy aggro.</p>

<p>A quick read on who an enemy is paying attention to, using Blizzard's own nameplate health bars. LH NameplateThreatColors adds role-aware threat colors without replacing the nameplates.</p>

<h2 style="color: #73c9c4;">What the colors mean</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Tank mode:</strong> Green for secure aggro, orange when it is threatened and red when a group member or pet has aggro.</li>
  <li><strong>DPS / Healer mode:</strong> Red when you have aggro, orange for high threat and green otherwise.</li>
  <li><strong>Automatic mode:</strong> Uses your assigned group role or specialization when available.</li>
</ul>

<p>The colors apply to attackable NPCs in combat while you are grouped or have a living pet. Outside combat, and during solo play without a living pet, Blizzard's colors remain in place. Tapped NPCs keep their gray priority.</p>

<h2 style="color: #c78be6;">Settings</h2>

<p>Open <strong>Options → AddOns → LH Tweaks → LH NameplateThreatColors</strong> to enable the colors and choose a role mode. The choice is saved per character. Blizzard nameplates need to be visible.</p>
